The courses

01

Doctrine of the Church

2 CREDITS
Monday & Wednesday · 12:00 – 1:00 PM

Doctrine of the Church is a comprehensive biblical study of the Church's purpose, place, and significance. It provides a theological foundation for understanding the value of the Church, both in its historical context as well as in today's world.

02

Pentateuch

3 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Pentateuch is the study of the first five books of the Bible: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy. This course will help you understand how these books relate to Old Testament history and the eternal purpose of God.

03

Discover Purpose or Know God

2 CREDITS
Know God: Monday · 9:00 – 10:00 AM | Discover Purpose: Tuesday · 1:30 – 2:30 PM

Discover Purpose is a personal journey helping you grow in your understanding of the gifts and calling God has placed on your life. Know God is a group study that focuses on learning to hear God’s voice and do what He’s asking you to do.

04

Homiletics

2 CREDITS
Monday · 9:00 – 11:00 AM

In this course the student is taught the process of building and utilizing outlines, texts, illustrations, word studies, background information, and dramatic dialogues for effectively preaching the Word of God.

05

Human Development

3 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 12:00 – 1:00 PM

A course dealing with the roles of men and women in society and how the individual functions within the group process. Strong emphasis is placed upon the necessity and dynamics of strong interpersonal relationships in all areas of life.

06

Building Leaders and Team

2 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Students will develop leadership as it applies to both individuals and teams. Learn principles and methods of training leaders as well as developing the dynamics of leadership teams.

07

Passions and Values

1 CREDIT
Monday · 10:00 – 11:00 AM

Passions and Values emphasize the devotional disciplines that should be a part of every Christian’s life. Each passion and value is developed fully so that the student is equipped to apply them to their lives.

08

Practicum

1 CREDIT
Wednesday · 1:00 & 2:00 PM

Each student will serve one hour per week in a strategic ministry area, learning firsthand from the pastoral staff what it means to build the local church, love people well, and lead with excellence.
