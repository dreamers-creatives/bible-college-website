The courses

01

Covenants

3 CREDITS
Monday · 1:00 – 2:00 PM | Wednesday · 1:00 – 3:00 PM

Covenants is an in-depth study of the nine biblical covenants God made with humanity, from the Edenic covenant through the everlasting covenant. These covenants are one of the main interpretive clues to unlocking the whole Bible and seeing how Scripture fits together.

02

Holy Spirit

2 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

This course builds a deep understanding of the theology of the Holy Spirit, from His work in the Old and New Testaments to Spirit baptism, spiritual gifts, and the fruit of the Spirit. Students also learn to apply it practically to a Spirit-filled life and calling.

03

Local Church

2 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 12:00 – 1:00 PM

Local Church gives a deeper understanding of God’s overarching purpose for the local church. Students explore community life, eldership as a leadership structure, and what healthy, practical church life looks like in today’s world.

04

Decision Making

2 CREDITS
Tuesday & Thursday · 1:00 – 2:00 PM

A study of the principles and practices of making decisions, with emphasis on the basis for sound judgment. Students learn how to seek God’s will, follow a practical process for hard choices, and develop the wisdom needed for both personal and leadership decisions.

05

Social Responsibility

3 CREDITS
Wednesday · 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Online

Students are exposed, both practically and philosophically, to how they can apply their faith and values in the world around them. The course looks at how Christians can help shape institutions such as government, media, education, and the courts.

06

Personal Discipline

2 CREDITS
Monday · 9:30 – 11:30 AM

Personal Discipline is a practical, personal journey in developing the spiritual and life disciplines that lead to a productive, fulfilling life, including the devotional habits that sustain a calling.

07

Pulpit Ministry

2 CREDITS
Monday · 9:30 – 11:30 AM

Pulpit Ministry equips students for the work of the pulpit: preparing messages from Scripture, growing in the character and confidence to stand and speak, and learning from an experienced pastor what it takes to feed a congregation.

08

Find Community

2 CREDITS
Monday · 12:00 – 1:00 PM

Find Community is a group study focused on building real, lasting relationships and learning to live in biblical community with the people God has placed around you.

09

Make A Difference

2 CREDITS
Monday · 12:00 – 1:00 PM

Make A Difference is a group study that moves students from faith to action, helping them serve their neighbors and share Christ’s love in practical ways.

10

Practicum

1 CREDIT
Thursday · 10:00 – 11:00 AM

Each student will serve one hour per week in a hands-on ministry area, learning firsthand from the pastoral staff what it takes to build up the local church and serve it with excellence.
