Am I called
to ministry?

Many people feel a pull toward ministry but are not sure what it means. You do not have to figure it all out at once. Calling usually becomes clearer step by step, as you grow and serve.

APPLY FOR SPRING 2027 ASK A QUESTION

Every believer has a calling

Scripture calls every follower of Jesus to love God, love people and make disciples. Some are also called to vocational ministry: serving the church as their main work. Both are real callings, and both are worth preparing for.

Signs God may be leading you into ministry

  • A growing desire to serve people and see them grow in faith
  • Others recognizing gifts in you, like teaching, leading or caring for people
  • Joy and fruit when you serve, even when it is hard
  • A burden for a specific group: kids, students, a city, the lost
  • Peace when you pray about it over time

Questions to ask

What do trusted leaders see in me? Where have I seen fruit when I serve? What breaks my heart? Am I willing to grow in character, not just skill?

Next steps

Start serving where you are, talk with a pastor, and create space to study the Bible and pray. Training does not lock you into a role; it prepares you for whatever God opens next.
