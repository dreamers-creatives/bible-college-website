Why a faith-focused year makes sense

Many students head straight into college or work without a clear sense of who they are or what they believe. A year focused on Scripture, community and service builds a foundation that holds up when life gets busy.

What to look for

  • Real Bible teaching, not just inspiration
  • Hands-on serving in a local church
  • Mentorship from leaders who know you by name
  • Community with people moving in the same direction
  • A next step: the option to keep going if it is a fit

For parents

A gap year should be healthy and purposeful. Ask about the schedule, supervision, the church connection and what students do each week. Parents are welcome to visit and ask questions.

Start with one year at DBC

Dreamer's Bible College offers a 1-year pathway that works as a faith-focused gap year, with the option to continue to 2 or 4 years. Students study Scripture, serve at Dreamer's Church, and take two trips within the United States.

Dreamer's Bible College is a church-integrated Bible college in Austin, Texas, with 1-year, 2-year and 4-year pathways, full-time and part-time options, and ministry focuses in Creative, Youth, Worship and Kids. Full-time tuition is $2,800 a semester with textbooks included.
