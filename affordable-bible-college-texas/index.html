TUITION & COST

What does
Bible college cost?

Cost is one of the first questions families ask. At Dreamer's Bible College in Austin, full-time tuition is a flat $2,800 per semester, with all required textbooks included.

APPLY FOR SPRING 2027 ASK A QUESTION

Dreamer's Bible College tuition

  • Full-time (12 to 16 credit hours): $2,800 per semester, flat rate. Credits beyond 16 hours are $200 each.
  • Part-time: $250 per credit hour, with at least 50% in-person attendance and up to 50% online, subject to course availability and approval.
  • Textbooks: all required textbooks and course materials are included.

Questions to ask any Bible college

  • What is included in tuition, and what costs extra?
  • Are there housing, fee or trip costs?
  • What scholarships or payment plans are available?
  • How much hands-on ministry experience will I get for the cost?

Scholarships and financial help

Scholarships or financial assistance are available at DBC depending on your pathway and enrollment. The best next step is to request tuition information and talk with admissions.

Talk with admissions about your options
