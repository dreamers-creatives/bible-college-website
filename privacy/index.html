Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Dreamer's Bible College ("DBC," "we," "us") is a ministry of Dreamer's Church in Austin, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect through dreamersbiblecollege.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you register for Preview Day, request information, ask a question or apply, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, address and any questions you send. These forms are provided by our student information system, Populi, and by VisitorReach, our church visitor follow-up tool. Each has its own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. These include:

  • Google Ads, to measure when someone who clicked one of our ads registers or requests information.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to measure and improve our ads on Facebook and Instagram.

These services may collect your IP address, browser type, pages visited and similar data. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings and in your Facebook ad preferences, or block cookies in your browser settings.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and send visit or Preview Day details
  • To follow up about programs, admissions and scholarships
  • To improve our website and measure how well our outreach works

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site, our forms and our admissions process, and only for those purposes, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in the email. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, contact us below.

Children

Our site is meant for prospective students, parents and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
